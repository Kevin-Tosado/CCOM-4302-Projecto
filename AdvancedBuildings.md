Story:

  Many years ago, deep within the vast Mayan jungle, there was an ancient city that grew and prospered every year. Its inhabitants believed that the gods had blessed them because their crops were abundant, trees grew all around the city, and vegetation covered most of their land. The city continued to expand, and its people built great temples and structures to honor the different gods they worshipped. Among the most important structures were the Temple of the Foliated Cross and El Caracol, where the inhabitants held ceremonies and observed the events in the sky. To them, the prosperity of their city was a sign that the gods were pleased with their people and that nothing could change their destiny.

  However, after many years of prosperity, a great drought struck the city. The sky remained clear for weeks and then months, without a single drop of rain. The rivers and small sources of water began to dry up, the crops stopped growing, and the animals began to disappear from the surrounding areas. Slowly, the trees lost their leaves, and the vegetation that had surrounded the city for generations began to die. The inhabitants tried to perform ceremonies and ask the gods they had worshipped for so many years for help, but the rain never came. The city that had once been full of life began to become a dry and silent place. When the people were about to lose all hope, a mysterious being named Shin appeared. Without asking for anything in return, Shin caused the clouds to cover the sky, and eventually, rain began to fall. The rain continued for days, filling the water sources again and bringing life back to the trees, plants, and animals surrounding the city.

  After witnessing this miracle, the inhabitants began to see Shin as a god sent to save them. They built temples and statues in his honor and gathered together to pray and thank him for bringing life back to their land. Unlike the other gods, Shin never asked for sacrifices, riches, or anything in return for his help, which made the people admire and trust him even more. Over time, Shin stopped being simply the savior of the city and became its leader and the only god worshipped by its people. However, no one knew why Shin had appeared during the drought or what his true purpose was. Now, years after his arrival, a new adventure begins when mysterious events start occurring around the city and its ancient temples, leading the player to discover who Shin truly is and why he chose to save the people of this ancient Mayan city.
